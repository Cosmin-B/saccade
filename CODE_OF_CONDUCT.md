# Code of Conduct

Saccade's project spaces are for precise, constructive work on the software. Participants
must treat one another with respect, discuss technical disagreement in good faith, and
make room for different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected conduct

- Keep criticism specific to code, behavior, evidence, or documented decisions.
- Provide enough context for another person to reproduce a claim.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and requests to redact personal information.
- Accept maintainer decisions without harassment or repeated disruption.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure
of private information, impersonation, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.
Spam, issue flooding, and knowingly misleading security or performance claims are also
outside the project's standards.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other official
project communication. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock a conversation, reject
a contribution, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, private where possible, and based on the
behavior rather than a person's viewpoint or identity.

Report GitHub-hosted abuse through GitHub's reporting tools. For a private project-specific
report, contact the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile.
Do not publish personal information or an unpatched vulnerability while reporting conduct.
